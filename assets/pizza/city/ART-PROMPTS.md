# Italian city artwork

Generated on 2026-10-10 with the built-in ImageGen tool. The user-provided Italian city reference informed the art direction; its interface and lettering are not baked into the game artwork.

## Runtime assets

- `italian-buildings-v2.png`: eight houses and pizzerias, 1536 by 1024, RGBA.
- `italian-landmarks-v2.png`: Colosseum and cathedral, 1774 by 887, RGBA.
- `italian-props-v2.png`: trees, fountain, bench, planter and one complete terrace table with chairs, 1536 by 1024, RGBA.

Each object is an independent flat 2D sprite. Atlas frames are isolated in `pizza-city-art.js`, rasterized once at the city's pixel scale and rendered with nearest-neighbor sampling. The original atlases remain intact. The city uses the same fixed 64 by 32 isometric grid for its roads, placements, traffic and depth order. There is no 3D scene, mesh or perspective camera.

UI icons, street paving, railings and directional car bitmaps are original code-native artwork.
