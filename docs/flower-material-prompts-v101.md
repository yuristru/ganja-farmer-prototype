## Shared final prompt

Use case: product-mockup. One single premium photorealistic dried cannabis flower cutout, large focus-stacked macro product photograph for a mobile game harvest reward. The whole flower fully inside the frame with 12 percent transparent margin on every side. Dense irregular naturally knobbly 3D calyx clusters, rounded asymmetric broad shape, approximately 1.5 times as tall as wide, slight lean. [FAMILY MATERIAL]. Very detailed rough frosty trichome surface, short curled orange hairs rooted in the flower, very few tiny tightly trimmed sugar leaves. Soft neutral studio key light from upper left, deep small occlusion shadows, lifelike rich restrained natural colors. Fully opaque flower on genuinely transparent alpha background, clean natural edges. No red or yellow chroma key fringe, no detached fragments, no floating tips, no stem, no leaves outside the main mass, no shadow on a floor, no grid, no jar, no labels, no text, no cartoon, no pinecone pattern, no smooth plastic scales. Subject fills about 75 percent of image height. Create a complete premium botanical macro, not a drawing.

## Family material substitutions

- Dream: sage and olive green, subtle cream resin and copper-orange curled pistils.
- Comet: fresh lime and muted golden green, abundant fine ivory resin and amber-orange pistils.
- Violet: deep plum purple calyx folds mixed with sage green, pale crystalline resin and copper pistils.
