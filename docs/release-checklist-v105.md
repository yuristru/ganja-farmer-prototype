# Proposed launch completion checklist after V105

This is the remaining acceptance work recommended for launch, not a claim that each area has a known defect.

- [ ] Conduct a full first-player playthrough: seed choice, daily care, waiting, upgrade purchase, harvest and collection. Add a short first-grow tutorial where users need it.
- [ ] Balance multiple consecutive grows across all 15 cultivars and care extremes: seed-pack prices, upgrade progression, daily coins, quality and yield rewards.
- [ ] Check performance and memory on a physical iPhone: plant interaction, high-tier equipment, repeated harvests and the 100-flower comparison, including background/resume.
- [ ] Review representative flowers from the 100-variant page: silhouette, tip continuity, extreme density/color expression and collection readability.
- [ ] Decide the launch save-recovery requirement and provide a tested export/import or another recovery mechanism if required.
- [ ] Finish launch delivery: app name/icon, installation path, production/debug menu policy and a release smoke test on target devices.

Completed in this session: 15 cultivars and 60 phenotypes, seed/care harvest demo, 100-variant comparison, corrected store art for all seven upgrade categories and 60 tiers. Automated checks are recorded in the corresponding V102 through V105 workflows; the real-device and player acceptance items above are still to be done.
